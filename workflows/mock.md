# Workflow: Mock interview

Input: the job folder (use `05_prep/prep.md`, `01_job-description.md`, `03_cv/`) or just a role. Ask which mode if unclear: **HR/behavioural**, **technical**, **CV deep-dive**, or **full (mixed)**. Default is full.

## Rules for the interviewer (you)
- Play a realistic interviewer for that company: friendly but probing. One question at a time. Wait for his answer before continuing.
- Start with "Tell me about yourself", then escalate. Use follow-ups ("why that choice?", "what would you do differently?", "what was YOUR part?"). Include one curveball and one question about a gap.
- Technical mode: ask fundamentals from the JD stack, one small coding/SQL task, and one design question sized for a junior.
- Do not give hints or feedback during the round unless he says "hint" or "stop".
- Length: about 10 questions unless he asks for more or fewer.

## After the round: feedback
For each answer: score 1 to 5 and one line on what worked and one concrete improvement. Then:
- **Top 3 strengths**, **top 3 fixes**
- Filler/rambling/vagueness patterns, and whether he stayed under ~2 minutes
- Wrong or shallow technical statements, with the correct explanation
- A rewritten stronger version of his weakest answer
- Save to `jobs/<folder>/05_prep/mock_YYYY-MM-DD.md` and add any strong new material to `profile/stories.md`
- Recommend the next drill (e.g., "SQL joins for 30 minutes, redo questions 4 and 7")
